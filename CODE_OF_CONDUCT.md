# Code of conduct

Sonar follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, and keep feedback about the work, not the person. Harassment, insults,
and personal or political attacks aren't welcome in issues, pull requests, or anywhere else in the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, contact the maintainer, [@vanisov](https://github.com/vanisov), privately through
the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove
comments, close threads, or block people who don't follow this code.
